Research Finding 2: The Compound Effect

Research Finding 3: Neuroplasticity and Learning
The brain's ability to rewire itself (neuroplasticity) means that with practice, Relaxation becomes easier and more natural over time. This is why consistency matters more than intensity.

Studies show that people who engage with a community while practicing Relaxation achieve better results and are more likely to stick with it long-term.
